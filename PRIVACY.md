# SafeLens Privacy

SafeLens analyzes camera frames and selected photos locally on the user's device.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Camera and Photo Access

Camera access is used for real-time lens reflection checking.

Photo library access is used when the user chooses an image for local analysis.

Selected photos and camera frames are processed on-device and are not uploaded to a server.

## Reports

The report helper creates text from information typed by the user. The generated report remains on the device unless the user chooses to save or share it outside the app.
